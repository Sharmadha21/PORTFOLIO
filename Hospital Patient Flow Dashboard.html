02 / Product decisions

Designed around the huddle.

The command center prioritizes a small set of operational decisions: where capacity is tightening, why the model thinks so, and what a human could do next.

The problem

ED boarding and delayed discharges create avoidable cost, congestion, and care-quality risk. Teams often see the bottleneck only after it has formed because bed, staffing, and discharge signals live in separate systems.

Primary users

Built for the people coordinating capacity, coverage, and escalation in real time.

Bed managerNursing supervisorHospital administrator

Success metrics

  • ED boarding hours per admitted patient
  • Left-without-being-seen rate
  • Average discharge completion time

Clinical & data guardrails

  • Predictions are advisory; a human approves every action
  • No patient-level clinical recommendations
  • HIPAA-safe aggregated operations data only
  • Show rationale, confidence, and data freshness

What production needs

  • EHR and HL7/FHIR integration for admissions, transfers, and discharge milestones
  • Real-time bed management, staffing, transport, and environmental services feeds
  • Role-based access, audit logs, data-quality monitoring, and downtime workflows
  • Site-specific model calibration, bias review, and a controlled pilot before scale
03 / Evaluation plan

How I would evaluate it.

Model performance is necessary, but the pilot succeeds only if teams trust the alerts and patient flow actually improves.

01

Forecast accuracy

Measure MAPE on hourly unit occupancy and error by horizon, unit, shift, and day type. Compare against a simple historical baseline.

MAPE = mean(|actual − forecast| / actual)
02

Alert quality

Measure precision and recall against retrospectively labeled surge events. Tune the threshold to reduce alert fatigue without missing meaningful events.

Track false alarms + missed surges by unit
03

Operational outcomes

Run a time-bounded pilot in selected units. Test whether boarding hours, discharge timing, and left-without-being-seen rate improve versus baseline.

Primary question: did boarding hours fall?
